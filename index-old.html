ACME Fertility & Healthcare Centre

Over a decade of building families in Imphal

Choosing where to start a family is a big decision, and you should know who you are trusting with it. ACME brings over a decade of experience to Imphal, more than 300 IVF and ICSI procedures, and a Manyata certification that confirms our care meets FOGSI’s quality standards.

Your first visit is a conversation. Our doctor goes through your history, explains your tests in plain words, and sets out your options, and you decide what comes next. Fertility treatment, obstetrics and gynaecology all sit under one roof, so you never have to start your story again.
