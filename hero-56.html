56 landing directions — every one grounded in a named style system

Built with assets/day-building.jpg, your real daytime photo. Each mockup maps to one of the 79 style systems in the ui-ux-pro-max dataset — the grey label on each card names it — so these are deliberate systems rather than 56 improvisations. 420px frames, two per row, so each is judgeable.

One measurement that shaped all of this. Your daytime photo has a luminance of 0.40. That means dark text sits at 7.9:1 on it and passes comfortably, while white text is only 2.32:1 and fails — the exact reverse of the night photo. So the light, ink-on-image treatments below are not just a taste call; they are the ones the photograph actually supports. Where white type appears I have put a scrim of at least 35% behind it, which brings it to 4.75:1.
